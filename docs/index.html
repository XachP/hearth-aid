🔥 Hearth

Dignified, confidential cash aid. Donors see a provable total and every round that was paid. An auditor reconciles every unit. Nobody can read what any family received, because on a public ledger a grant size can reveal a family's size, illness or protection status.

🧾 Donors trust the booksFunds raised are public and provable. The auditor's view key opens every grant and the treasury, so Σ grants + treasury = funds raised, with no power to spend.
🛡️ Families stay safeGrants are Pedersen commitments. No amount appears in state, events or receipts. The template rejects any grant that would reveal one.
🚫 No double-dipping, no PIIEach household is enrolled with a salted hash of its registration ID. A second wallet with the same hash is refused on-chain. No names, no IDs.
📱 Lost phone? Keep your placereplace_wallet moves an enrolment to a new phone without creating a second entitlement.
